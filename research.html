Research / Evidence & assumptions

A future grounded
in present signals.

The appendix behind the experience. Separate observed changes, experimental materials and the assumptions we introduced to build our 2035 world.

EVIDENCE

What exists.

Published outage data, device dependence, preparedness advice and demonstrations of energy harvesting materials.

SCENARIO

What we imagine.

Recurring restricted charging periods, informal reserve trading and a new Herschel energy category in 2035.

TO VALIDATE

What remains open.

Usable daily energy, weight, comfort, safety, durability, repair cost and whether people want this trade-off.

Three layers. Different jobs.

LayerRole in the conceptResearch boundary
Paper-thin solar filmMIT-inspired exterior energy collectionFabric-mounted cells showed high power per mass; protection and integration still matter.
Solar yarnExplore collecting light within the fabricNottingham Trent prototype reported about 400 mW. It does not establish full backpack output.
Energy storageRemovable reserve initially; battery lining as a future research directionMIT battery fibers store energy. They do not generate it, and a complete battery lining is unvalidated.
Kinetic & thermal researchExplore motion and temperature differences as other inputsNot selected as proven phone charging systems. Energy output, comfort and gradients need testing.
Power is not energy. A 0.4 W prototype operating at constant output for five hours produces 2 Wh before losses. This is a simple calculation, not a backpack performance claim. Sunlight, orientation, exposed area and charging electronics all affect actual results.
