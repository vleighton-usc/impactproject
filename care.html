STUDENT CONCEPT
Care & repair / Proposed service experience

Keep the bag.
Change what needs changing.

A durable textile shell should not become disposable because a port, battery or collection component wears out. This is the repair system our future product would need.

CONCEPT SERVICE · These are proposed design principles, not tested care instructions, available repair services or actual Herschel warranty terms.

Removable reserve.

Separate stored energy from the bag for replacement and service. Compatibility across future bags is a design goal requiring engineering validation.

Serviceable connections.

Make ports and connections accessible to qualified repair partners instead of permanently trapping them inside the shell.

A longer textile life.

Design the fabric, stitching and compartments to remain useful while the energy components evolve.

A proposed support flow

What needs attention?

Try a sample support decision. This demo does not diagnose hardware, collect personal details or book a repair.

Proposed route: a qualified repair partner checks the connector and replaces the serviceable port module if needed. Keep the textile shell.

Questions to resolve before launch.

How long should the system last?

The presentation’s interview feedback makes longevity a priority. The team would need abrasion, flex, weather exposure, electrical and lifecycle testing before setting a lifetime or warranty claim.

Can the whole bag go in a washing machine?

Not established. A washable laboratory textile does not make the complete backpack machine washable. Final care requirements depend on all layers, wiring, electronics and seals.

Can I open or modify the power module?

The concept favors professionally serviceable modules, rather than asking users to alter battery cells or electrical protection systems. Community customization can focus on non-electrical storage and organization.

What happens at end of life?

The proposed system separates textile repair, electronic component recovery and battery recycling. Actual collection partners, logistics and costs remain development questions.
